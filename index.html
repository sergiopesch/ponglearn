Inspired by DeepMind

The principle behind AlphaGo

DeepMind's AlphaGo showed the power of learning from experience and reward. This project does not recreate AlphaGo. It borrows the teaching principle in a smaller form: a Pong paddle builds a habit from repeated attempts. The paper describes policy estimates for choosing moves, value estimates for judging positions, reinforcement learning from self-play, and search guided by those estimates.

AlphaGo complex board positions, move choices, long-term outcomes
PongLearn ball position, paddle moves, immediate hits and misses

    DeepMind Principle

    adapted

    Silver et al. describe AlphaGo as combining policy networks that select moves, value networks that evaluate positions, reinforcement learning from self-play, and search guided by those estimates. PongLearn is a simplified teaching analogy: Pong states replace Go boards, paddle actions replace Go moves, and rewards from hits or misses update visible action values.

    Source: Mastering the game of Go with deep neural networks and tree search
